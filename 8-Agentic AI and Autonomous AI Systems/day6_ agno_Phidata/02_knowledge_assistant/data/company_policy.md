# NovaWorks Employee Travel Policy — Training Data

This document is fictional and exists only for the classroom demo.

## Domestic travel
Employees may book economy-class rail or air travel for approved domestic business trips. Flights should normally be booked at least 10 calendar days before departure. A department manager must approve bookings above INR 20,000 for a round trip.

## International travel
Economy class is the default for international flights. Premium economy may be approved when the scheduled one-way flight duration exceeds 8 hours. Business class requires written approval from the Chief Operating Officer and is normally limited to a scheduled one-way flight duration exceeding 12 hours.

## Hotels
The standard hotel reimbursement cap is INR 8,000 per night within India, EUR 160 per night in continental Europe, GBP 160 per night in the United Kingdom, and USD 190 per night in the United States. Taxes may be reimbursed in addition to the cap when shown separately on the invoice.

## Meals
For travel within India, employees may claim up to INR 1,500 per day for meals. For international travel, the standard meal allowance is EUR 55, GBP 50, or USD 65 per day depending on destination currency. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## Local transport
Public transport, airport shuttles, taxis, and app-based ride services may be reimbursed when reasonably required for business travel. A receipt is required for an individual local-transport expense above INR 1,000 or the local-currency equivalent.

## Expense submission
Expense reports must normally be submitted within 15 calendar days after returning from the trip. Employees should attach itemized receipts and the relevant business-trip approval.

## Exceptions
Any exception to the travel policy must be documented and approved by both the employee's department manager and Finance before the expense is incurred, except in emergencies where retrospective approval may be requested.
